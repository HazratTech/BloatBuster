# Shopify App Marketing & Growth Playbook

## 1. The Zero-to-50 Merchant Acquisition Blueprint
New Shopify apps struggle because the App Store algorithm suppresses apps with 0 reviews. You must manufacture early momentum using high-converting, manual growth loops:

### A. The "Free Store Speed Audit" Cold Outreach Funnel
1. **Targeting:**
   - Use builtwith.com or storeleads.app to find Shopify stores running 15+ apps.
   - Run BloatBuster's storefront scanner against their homepage domain (`yourbrand.com`).
2. **Personalized Outreach Script (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, or Contact Form):**
   > *"Hey [Name], I noticed your store [Brand] has great products, but while browsing your homepage I ran a quick script audit and saw that dead tracking tags from [OldApp1] and [OldApp2] are still downloading ~380KB of heavy JavaScript on every visitor's mobile load.
   > 
   > I built a lightweight tool called BloatBuster that safely isolates and deactivates these with 1 click without touching theme code.
   > 
   > I'd love to give you a 100% free account to clean this up and speed up your mobile checkout if you'd be willing to share honest feedback on the Shopify App Store. Let me know if you'd like the link!"*

### B. Community Seeding on Reddit (`r/shopify` & `r/ecommerce`)
* **Rule:** Never post a raw promotional link. Always provide a free diagnostic and direct technical value.
* **Winning Post Formula:**
  - **Title:** *"I built a free tool to audit leftover app code slowing down your theme (no catch, 100% free report)"*
  - **Body:** Explain that Shopify does not remove theme code when apps are uninstalled. Offer to run a free manual audit for any store owner who drops their domain in the comments.
  - Deliver instant value: Post the exact file lines where orphaned scripts reside.
  - Store owners will naturally install the app to get automated 1-click cleanups and leave glowing reviews.

### C. Agency & Freelancer Partner Channel
* **The Opportunity:** Shopify theme developers and digital agencies hate manually hunting for ghost code in client themes.
* **The Offer:** Give Shopify agencies free "Partner Access" to BloatBuster to audit their client stores during redesigns.
* **The Return:** Agencies install BloatBuster on dozens of client stores, providing high-retention recurring subscriptions.

## 2. Pricing Psychology: The $2.99 "Impulse Protection" Model
Competitors like GhostCode charge **$29/month**, creating massive friction:
* At $29/mo, merchants hesitate, run a single scan, clean their theme, and immediately cancel. Churn is brutal.
* At **$2.99/month**, BloatBuster falls below the merchant's "cancellation threshold." It costs less than a single coffee.
* **Positioning:** Don't market BloatBuster merely as a one-time cleaner. Market it as **"Continuous Theme Speed Insurance & 24/7 Watchdog"**:
  - Automatically backs up themes before any app installation.
  - Alerts store owners whenever uninstalled apps leave garbage behind.
  - Maintains Grade A storefront speed month after month.
  - Merchants leave the $2.99/mo subscription running indefinitely.

## 3. Review Acceleration Framework
To reach "Built for Shopify" and rank in top category rows:
* Target: 5 verified 5-star reviews within the first 30 days.
* Rule of thumb: 1 review per 5 satisfied active merchants.
* Deliver high-touch customer support: Offer live theme inspection assistance in the app chat or support email (`support@relayworks.dev`). A merchant who receives personal help from the developer will write a 5-star review 90% of the time.
